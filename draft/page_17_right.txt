\section*{20. The Midsegment}

If $S$ is the midpoint of $AB$, and $P$ is the midpoint of $AC$,
then $SP \parallel BC$, and $SP = \frac{1}{2} BC$.
The converse is also true:
If $S$ is the midpoint of $AB$, and $SP \parallel BC$,
then $P$ is the midpoint of $AC$, and $SP = \frac{1}{2} BC$.

\subsection*{Example 58}
In the drawn triangle, what is the length of $SP$?
\begin{enumerate}[A.]
    \item 15
    \item 20
    \item 5
    \item 10
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Solution:}
$SP$ is a midsegment in the triangle.
Length of $SP = \frac{1}{2} \times 10 = 5$.

\subsection*{Example 59}
If $AB = 4$, $AC = 3$.
Find $DH$.
\begin{enumerate}[A.]
    \item 1.5
    \item 2
    \item 2.5
    \item 3
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Solution:}
$m(\angle A) = m(\angle H) = 90^\circ$, which means $AC \parallel HD$.
Since $H$ is the midpoint of $AB$,
then $DH = \frac{1}{2} AC$, which means $DH = 1.5$.

\section*{21. Congruence in Triangles}

If $\triangle ACB \cong \triangle DWH$, then:
\begin{enumerate}[(i)]
    \item $AC = DW$, $AB = DH$, $CB = WH$
    \item $m(\angle A) = m(\angle D)$, $m(\angle C) = m(\angle W)$, $m(\angle B) = m(\angle H)$
\end{enumerate}
