\subsection*{34. What is the value of $\frac{1}{10} + \frac{1}{100} + \frac{1}{1000}$?}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item[a)] $0.111$
    \item[b)] $0.0111$
    \item[c)] $0.12$
    \item[d)] $1110$
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Solution:}
$\frac{1}{10} = 0.1$ \\
$\frac{1}{100} = 0.01$ \\
$\frac{1}{1000} = 0.001$ \\
The expression becomes $0.1 + 0.01 + 0.001$. \\
We add vertically to get the result $0.111$ (b)
\begin{align*}
    &0.1 \\
    &0.01 \\
+ \quad &0.001 \\
\cline{2-2}
    &0.111
\end{align*}
